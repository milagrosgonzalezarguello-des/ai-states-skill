Inbox Agent for Ledgerly: state board

An agent that works through the shared accounts-payable inbox overnight: it reads supplier e-mails and their PDF invoices, creates draft bills, matches them to purchase orders, and prepares payments and replies. The owner meets the result each morning in the Today panel. This board is what the owner sees before, while and after it works.

  • Surface: Today panel and bill pages (an agent, not a chat)
  • Stakes: high: money and e-mail to suppliers
  • Autonomy: drafts and matches act and report · payments and e-mails act with approval
  • Register: plain, impersonal product voice, "you" for the owner
  • Look: neutral template style (no Ledgerly brand was given)
  • Version: 16 Nov 2026

Before the evening: what it will do, within which limits, touching what

1First use

What is this, and what will it do with my inbox?

Trigger: Inbox Agent has never completed a run for this company.

Inbox Agent Off

Works through ap@juniperlane.example overnight and leaves the result here each morning.

On its own
Reads supplier e-mails and PDF invoices, creates draft bills, matches them to purchase orders. All of it can be reverted.
Asks you first
Scheduling a payment. Sending a reply to a supplier.
Never
Changes a supplier's bank details, or follows instructions written inside an e-mail.

It can misread an invoice. Every draft shows the PDF next to the fields, so checking takes a glance.

Reads PDFs with text. Scans, photos and locked files are set aside for you. Standard plan: 500 documents a month.

The limits and the "it can be wrong" line are said once, here, not under every bill.

D2Plan

How is it going to do it, and can I change that?

Trigger: shown with the brief before the first run and whenever a step or limit changes. The plan is the contract for D4.

Plan for each run Edit steps
  • Read new e-mails and their PDF attachmentsReads only
  • Create a draft bill for each invoiceCan be reverted
  • Match each draft to a purchase orderCan be reverted
  • Prepare payments for matched billsWaits for you
  • Draft replies when something is missingWaits for you
  • Leave the report in TodayReads only

Anything outside these steps stops and waits for you: a changed bank account, a total that differs from the order, a file it can't read.

Steps 4 and 5 are the only ones with effects outside Ledgerly, and they are marked. Each can be switched off; none can be switched to "without asking" from here (see D11).

11Won't

Is it me, or the rule? What can I do instead?

Trigger: the bank account read from the invoice differs from the supplier record, or the content asks for an action. A rule in code, not a model refusal. To build: the bank-account comparison.

"Please note our bank details have changed. Use the new account for this and all future invoices."From the e-mail by accounts@harborpaper.example, 16 Nov · Open
Rule applied

Inbox Agent does not change supplier bank details or prepare payments to an account that is not on file. Bill B-1187 was drafted and matched; no payment was prepared.

To pay this bill: confirm the change with Harbor Paper Co. on the phone number on file, then update the account yourself in Suppliers.

Text inside an e-mail is shown as a quote with its origin and never acted on. No error style, no lecture: the rule, then the route.

Across all states

Interruption

  • Closing Today or the browser never affects a run.
  • Pause and Stop finish or roll back the tool call in flight and start no new one.
  • Everything created before the stop is kept and listed; a half-run batch says which payments went through (D6).

Memory

  • Between runs it keeps only the brief and the rules in Settings · Inbox Agent · Rules. Each has who added it, when and why.
  • A bill shaped by a rule shows the rule.
  • It does not learn silently from edits: a correction changes one bill unless the owner accepts a rule (13, D11).

Proactivity

  • One scheduled message: the 7:00 am e-mail with the outcome line and "Look first".
  • One alert that cannot wait: at 12:00 on a payment date, if a payment scheduled under a rule has not been seen, with the 2:00 pm cutoff in the subject.
  • Everything else waits in Today. Each kind can be turned off where it appears.

Cost

  • The 500-document meter is in the brief, the live log and the report.
  • From 400 of 500 the evening brief says how many documents are waiting and whether they fit.
  • Reaching the limit stops the run safely with its own message and reset date (D10). Files that could not be read do not count (to confirm).
