Ledgerly Inbox Agent: state board

Overnight, Inbox Agent reads the shared accounts-payable inbox, turns supplier invoices into draft bills, matches them to purchase orders, and prepares payments and supplier replies. The owner reviews everything in the Today panel in the morning.

  • Surface: background agent + Today panel (web, checked at phone width)
  • Stakes: high (money, e-mail to suppliers, irreversible actions)
  • Autonomy: drafts and matches on its own; payments and replies only after approval
  • Voice: impersonal product voice, plain English, no persona
  • Look: neutral template style (no Ledgerly brand given)
  • Version: 17 Nov 2026, new design

Coverage

23 of 23 states that apply are designed. 3 answer states do not apply as separate states (their delegation counterpart replaces them); the sensitive-moment card does not apply while the product has no free-text box.

Before setting it up and agreeing the nightly job

1First use

What is this and what can it do?

Trigger: Inbox Agent not set up for this business (no connected inbox, no run yet).

Today Inbox Agent: not set up

Let Inbox Agent sort your supplier invoices overnight.

  • Reads new e-mails and PDF invoices in your accounts-payable inbox
  • Creates draft bills and matches them to purchase orders
  • Prepares payments and replies to suppliers for you to approve
Nothing goes out on its own

No payment is scheduled and no e-mail is sent until you approve it here. Drafts and matches can be reverted.

Can't read password-protected files or invoices inside .zip files. Your plan includes 500 documents a month.

The "can't read" limits are said here so owners learn them before the first failure (state 10).

D3Permission

What exactly am I letting it touch, and for how long?

Trigger: (a) set-up: the mailbox is not connected yet. (b) the owner has approved payments to the same supplier 5 times without changes. To confirm: the threshold of 5.

a. Connecting the inbox

Connect your accounts-payable inbox

Inbox Agent will be able to:

  • Read e-mails and attachments in accounts@maplestreetbakery.example
  • Add the label "Ledgerly" to e-mails it has processed

It won't delete or move e-mails, and it won't send from this inbox without your approval of each reply.

b. Offer to stop asking (earned, never default)

Schedule some payments without asking?

You approved the last 5 payments to each of Northwind Flour Co., Greenfield Produce and Pinecrest Paper without changes.

Inbox Agent could schedule their payments on its own when all of these are true: the bill matches the PO exactly, the bank details are the ones on file, and the amount is $2,000.00 or less.

Each one would still appear in Today, and can be cancelled until 2:00 pm on the payment date. Expires in 90 days unless you renew it.

Across all states

Interruption

  • Pause finishes the document in flight, then stops; Stop does the same and hands back a report (D6).
  • A run never stops halfway through a write: a draft bill is created whole or not at all.
  • Closing Today never affects a run.

Memory

  • Only rules the owner confirms (D11), each with date and origin, removable.
  • A bill says when a rule shaped it.
  • No hidden learning from approvals; the offer to stop asking is explicit (D3b) and expires after 90 days.

Proactivity

  • One notification at 6:00 am with the outcome line and the number of decisions.
  • An extra notification only for a held payment due within 24 hours, or a bank-change request.
  • Notification kinds can be turned off from the notification itself.

Cost

  • Documents used this month shown in D2, D4 and settings ("456 of 500").
  • From 450 used: tonight's plan (D2) adds "44 documents left this month; 12 e-mails waiting so far".
  • At 500 the run stops safely (D10); the untouched e-mails stay in the inbox.
