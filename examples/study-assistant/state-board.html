Study Assistant: state board

A chat in the student portal that answers questions about courses, deadlines and regulations from the university's official documents. About 12,000 students; most screens are drawn at phone width because many use it on their phones.

  • Surface: chat in the student portal, phone first
  • Stakes: medium; high for deadlines and rules that affect progression
  • Autonomy: answers only, takes no actions
  • Register: English, plain, "you" (to confirm)
  • Version: 17 Nov 2026, first design

Coverage

13 states apply: 8 designed, 5 designed with a signal to confirm or build. 1 does not apply. The sensitive moment is designed and waits for review by Counselling and Campus Security.

Sensitive moment a student writes about harm, danger or not coping instead of asking a question

SSensitive moment

Is anyone going to take this seriously, and who do I go to right now?

Trigger: a classifier on the student's message, separate from the refusal signal and run before retrieval, set to catch too much rather than too little. It should catch: thoughts of suicide, self-harm or not wanting to be alive; being in danger or an emergency now (someone hurt, a threat, being followed); sexual harassment, assault, stalking or abuse, including by staff or other students; severe distress or not coping; threats to harm others. The card is fixed copy chosen by category and time of day, not generated, so it still appears when the model is down or a limit is reached. To build: the classifier and its categories. To confirm: every route, number and hour below.

No sources, no answer, no Helpful / Not helpful, no Regenerate, no suggested questions on any of these screens. Nothing is reported or opened on the student's behalf. The composer stays enabled: if the student goes on to a course question, the assistant answers it normally. The card does not say who can see the conversation, because that is not known yet. A "your question is still here" row is added below the card in every variant when the message also contains a question.

Review with the Counselling team lead and Campus Security before shipping. Copy, order of routes and the out-of-hours line are proposals.

Across all states

Interruption

  • Stop keeps what was written, marked "Stopped · incomplete", with no sources or feedback (card 3).
  • Closing the portal or locking the phone mid-answer: the answer finishes on the server and is there when the student returns. To confirm.
  • A dropped connection is state 12b, with the question kept.

Memory

  • Inside a conversation: the "Answering for" level and faculty, and earlier questions.
  • Across conversations: nothing, unless the team decides otherwise. The first screen links to "Who can see this chat"; its content is to confirm with the Data Protection Office.
  • Never "I'll remember that".

Proactivity

  • Not applicable: it only answers. Deadline reminders, if wanted later, belong to the portal's notifications, with their own opt-in.

Cost

  • Free to students. If a per-student daily limit exists, it is a 12 screen with the reset time; the sensitive card and help sheet are exempt. To confirm.
